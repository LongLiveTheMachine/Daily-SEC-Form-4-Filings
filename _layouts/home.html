We Do the Filtering. You Make the Move.

InsiderIQ Pro tracks SEC insider filings every weekday and surfaces only the trades showing High Conviction insider signals — the ones that historically lead to the biggest moves.

01 // CLUSTER BUYS
Cluster Buys
Multiple insiders buying the same stock simultaneously — a powerful high-conviction insider signal of shared belief at the top.
02 // PREMIUM PRICE
High Insider Purchase Price
Insiders paying more than the current market price to get in. This screams urgency — they want a seat before the ride takes off.
03 // HIGH VOLUME
High Volume Buy
Purchase volumes that dwarf the stock's daily to 3-month average — context that separates a meaningful buy from a routine one.
04 // STREAK
Consecutive Buying
An insider buying over multiple days shows sustained commitment — a strong high-conviction insider signal, not a one-off decision.
05 // ESCALATING VOL
Escalating Volume
Consecutive buying where the number of shares increases with each trade — doubling down with growing conviction over time.
06 // ESCALATING PRICE
Escalating Conviction
Consecutive buying where the purchase price rises with each trade — an insider willing to pay more to keep accumulating.
How stacking works: The more high-conviction insider signals that appear together on a single trade, the stronger the setup. Stacking signals narrows your results — you'll see fewer trades, but each one carries far more conviction.
